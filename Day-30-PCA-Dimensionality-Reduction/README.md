# Day 30 – Principal Component Analysis (PCA)

Welcome to **Day 30** of my AI/ML Engineer Bootcamp journey! Today, I explored **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**, an unsupervised Machine Learning technique used for dimensionality reduction.

---

## 📌 Objective

Understand how PCA transforms high-dimensional data into fewer principal components while preserving as much variance as possible.

---

## 📚 Topics Covered

- ✅ Introduction to PCA
- ✅ Dimensionality Reduction
- ✅ Principal Components: PC1 and PC2
- ✅ Feature Scaling with StandardScaler
- ✅ Explained Variance and Explained Variance Ratio
- ✅ Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors
- ✅ Selecting the Number of Components
- ✅ Cumulative Explained Variance
- ✅ PCA Visualization
- ✅ PCA with Logistic Regression
- ✅ Data Leakage Prevention
- ✅ Model Evaluation

By the end of Day 30, I can:

---

## 💡 Why PCA?

PCA helps simplify high-dimensional datasets, reduce redundant information, and visualize complex data. It can also reduce model training time, although it does not guarantee improved accuracy.
