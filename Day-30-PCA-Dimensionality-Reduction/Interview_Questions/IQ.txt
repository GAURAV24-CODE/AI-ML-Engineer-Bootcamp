PCA INTERVIEW QUESTIONS — SHORT
Day 30 | AI/ML Engineer Bootcamp
====================================

Q1. What is PCA?
PCA reduces the number of features while preserving variance.

Q2. What is the full form of PCA?
Principal Component Analysis.

Q3. Is PCA supervised or unsupervised?
Unsupervised learning.

Q4. Why is PCA used?
For dimensionality reduction and data visualization.

Q5. What is PC1?
The component capturing maximum variance.

Q6. What is PC2?
The next-highest variance component, orthogonal to PC1.

Q7. Why is scaling important in PCA?
To prevent large-scale features from dominating.

Q8. What is explained variance?
The variance captured by a component.

Q9. What does PCA(n_components=2) do?
Reduces the data to two components.

Q10. What does PCA(n_components=0.95) mean?
Retains approximately 95% of the variance.

Q11. Does PCA use target labels?
No.

Q12. What is a major disadvantage of PCA?
Reduced interpretability and possible information loss.

Q13. PCA vs. feature selection?
PCA creates new features; feature selection keeps original features.

Q14. PCA vs. LDA?
PCA maximizes variance; LDA focuses on class separation.

Q15. Can PCA improve accuracy?
Sometimes, but not always.

Q16. Can PCA be used before Logistic Regression?
Yes.

Q17. How do you avoid data leakage with PCA?
Split first; fit PCA only on training data.

Q18. Are principal components correlated?
They are uncorrelated in standard PCA.

Q19. Is PCA sensitive to outliers?
Yes.

Q20. How do you choose the number of components?
Use cumulative explained variance and model evaluation.
