DAY 12 — PROBABILITY
INTERVIEW QUESTIONS & 1–2 LINE ANSWERS
AI/ML ENGINEER BOOTCAMP

1. What is Probability?
Probability measures how likely an event is to happen, from 0 (impossible) to 1 (certain).

2. What is a Sample Space?
A sample space is the complete set of all possible outcomes of an experiment.

3. What is an Event?
An event is a specific outcome or group of outcomes from a sample space.

4. What is the complement of an event?
The complement means the event does not occur. Formula: P(Aᶜ) = 1 − P(A).

5. What is the Addition Rule?
For mutually exclusive events, P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B).

6. What is the Multiplication Rule?
For independent events, P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B).

7. What is Conditional Probability?
It is the probability of an event occurring given that another event has already occurred: P(A|B) = P(A∩B)/P(B).

8. What are independent events?
Two events are independent when the occurrence of one does not affect the probability of the other.

9. What are dependent events?
Two events are dependent when the occurrence of one changes the probability of the other.

10. What is a Random Variable?
A random variable assigns numerical values to outcomes of a random experiment.

11. What is a Discrete Random Variable?
It takes countable values, such as number of customers or number of defects.

12. What is a Continuous Random Variable?
It can take any value within a range, such as height, weight, or temperature.

13. What is Expected Value?
Expected value is the long-run average value of a random variable.

14. What is Variance?
Variance measures how far values are spread from their mean.

15. What is a Probability Distribution?
A probability distribution describes the possible values of a variable and their probabilities.

16. What is Bernoulli Distribution?
It models one trial with exactly two outcomes, such as success/failure or yes/no.

17. What is Binomial Distribution?
It models the number of successes in a fixed number of independent Bernoulli trials.

18. What is Poisson Distribution?
It models the number of events occurring in a fixed interval when events happen at a known average rate.

19. What is Normal Distribution?
It is a continuous, symmetric, bell-shaped distribution centered around its mean.

20. What is the 68-95-99.7 rule?
For a normal distribution, about 68%, 95%, and 99.7% of values lie within 1, 2, and 3 standard deviations of the mean.

21. Why is probability important in Machine Learning?
It helps ML models handle uncertainty, estimate outcomes, classify data, and make predictions.

22. Where is probability used in ML?
It is used in classification, Naive Bayes, Logistic Regression, Bayesian methods, and uncertainty estimation.

23. What is the difference between Bernoulli and Binomial?
Bernoulli represents one trial; Binomial represents the number of successes across multiple fixed trials.

24. What is the difference between discrete and continuous variables?
Discrete variables are countable, while continuous variables can take any value within a range.

25. What is the difference between expected value and variance?
Expected value describes the average outcome, while variance describes the spread around that average.

QUICK INTERVIEW FORMULAS
P(A) = Favorable outcomes / Total outcomes
P(Aᶜ) = 1 − P(A)
P(A|B) = P(A∩B) / P(B)
Independent: P(A∩B) = P(A)P(B)
