DAY 27/45 - SVM INTERVIEW QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS

1. What is SVM?
SVM is a supervised machine learning algorithm used for classification and regression.

2. What are support vectors?
They are the data points closest to the decision boundary that help determine its position.

3. What is a hyperplane?
A hyperplane is a decision boundary used to separate different classes.

4. What is the margin in SVM?
The margin is the distance between the decision boundary and the nearest data points.

5. What is the difference between SVC and SVR?
SVC predicts classes, while SVR predicts continuous numerical values.

6. What is a kernel in SVM?
A kernel helps SVM learn complex, nonlinear patterns in data.

7. Name some common SVM kernels.
Linear, Polynomial, RBF, and Sigmoid.

8. What is the RBF kernel?
RBF is a popular kernel that helps SVM model nonlinear decision boundaries.

9. What does the C parameter do?
It controls the trade-off between a wider margin and penalizing training errors.

10. What is gamma in SVM?
Gamma controls how far the influence of individual training points extends for kernels such as RBF.

11. Why is feature scaling important in SVM?
Features with larger numerical ranges can disproportionately affect SVM's calculations.

12. Why use StandardScaler()?
It standardizes features to have approximately zero mean and unit variance.

13. Why use a pipeline in SVM?
A pipeline combines preprocessing and model training, applying transformations consistently.

14. What is epsilon in SVR?
It defines a tolerance range around predictions where errors are not penalized.

15. How do you evaluate SVC?
Common metrics include accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, and the confusion matrix.

16. How do you evaluate SVR?
Common metrics include MAE, MSE, RMSE, and R-squared.

17. What are the advantages of SVM?
It can perform well in high-dimensional spaces and model both linear and nonlinear patterns.

18. What are the disadvantages of SVM?
It can be slow on large datasets and sensitive to feature scaling and hyperparameter selection.

19. What is overfitting in SVM?
Overfitting occurs when a model learns training data too closely and performs poorly on unseen data.

20. How can you tune SVM hyperparameters?
Use methods such as GridSearchCV or RandomizedSearchCV with cross-validation.
