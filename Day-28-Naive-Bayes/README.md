# Day 28 – Naive Bayes

Welcome to **Day 28** of my AI/ML Engineer Bootcamp journey! Today, I explored **Naive Bayes**, a probabilistic Machine Learning algorithm based on Bayes' Theorem.

---

## 📌 Objective

Understand how Naive Bayes uses probability and feature independence assumptions to perform classification.

---

## 📚 Topics Covered

- ✅ Introduction to Naive Bayes
- ✅ Bayes' Theorem
- ✅ Conditional Probability
- ✅ Independence Assumption
- ✅ Gaussian Naive Bayes
- ✅ Multinomial Naive Bayes
- ✅ Bernoulli Naive Bayes
- ✅ Laplace Smoothing
- ✅ Model Training and Prediction
- ✅ Model Evaluation

## 💡 Why Naive Bayes?

Naive Bayes is simple, fast, and effective for many classification tasks. It is commonly used in spam detection, text classification, and sentiment analysis.
