# Day 25 – Decision Tree

Welcome to **Day 25** of my AI/ML Engineer Bootcamp journey! Today, I explored **Decision Trees**, supervised Machine Learning models that make predictions using a sequence of decision rules.

---

## 📌 Objectiv

Understand how Decision Trees split data into smaller groups to perform classification and regression.

---

## 📚 Topics Covered

- ✅ Introduction to Decision Trees
- ✅ Decision Tree Classification
- ✅ Decision Tree Regression
- ✅ Root Node, Decision Nodes, and Leaf Nodes
- ✅ Splitting Features
- ✅ Gini Impurity
- ✅ Entropy and Information Gain
- ✅ Model Training and Prediction
- ✅ Overfitting and Tree Depth
- ✅ Model Evaluation

## 💡 Why Decision Trees?

Decision Trees are easy to interpret and can model nonlinear relationships. They are used in customer segmentation, risk assessment, and many classification and regression problems.
